## 1. Curriculum Architecture & Pedagogical Framework

### 1.1 Philosophy & Core Design Principles
The **Pupil PH Basic English Curriculum** is an accelerated, high-engagement English language learning program specifically calibrated for Filipino learners across K-10 levels. It adheres strictly to five guiding pedagogical principles:

1. **Pure Simple English Immersion (No Translations)**:
   - Filipino learners encounter high levels of cognitive friction when forced to toggle between English and Tagalog definitions. 
   - Every definition, instructional prompt, story, and quiz explanation is written in **Pure Simple English** using high-frequency vocabulary and visual scaffolding. 
   - Abstract terminology is replaced with direct, tangible explanations.

2. **Everyday Philippine Cultural Grounding**:
   - Rather than foreign contexts (e.g., snow, subways, maple syrup), language acquisition happens faster when situated in the immediate sensory environment of the learner: mango trees, tricycles, jeepneys, local public markets, barangay basketball courts, tropical rain showers, and polite school community interactions.
   - Cultural nouns widely used in Philippine English (e.g., *jeepney*, *tricycle*, *sari-sari store*, *barangay*) are integrated naturally within Simple English sentences.

3. **DepEd MATATAG English Curriculum Alignment**:
   - Aligns directly with the Department of Education (DepEd) MATATAG English curriculum pillars: Phonological Awareness, Phonics and Word Study, Vocabulary Development, Grammar Awareness, and Comprehending Literary and Informational Texts.

4. **Dignified Remediation for Older Learners (Track 4A: Foundations Bridge)**:
   - High school learners with low English literacy feel alienated and demotivated by primary-grade imagery (e.g., cartoon animals, baby toys). 
   - **Track 4A** ("Start from Basics") provides foundational sight-word acquisition and basic sentence patterns using modern, dignified digital themes: navigating mobile phones, online school portals, registration forms, button actions (*Submit*, *Upload*, *Download*, *Confirm*, *Cancel*), and account security.

5. **Multi-Sensory Triad (Read - Hear - Build)**:
   - Every concept is reinforced through three touchpoints:
     1. **Visual/Iconic Card**: Graphic cue + clear phonetic guide + simple definition + contextual sentence.
     2. **Kinesthetic Sentence Builder**: Drag-and-drop ordered word tiles with distractors to reinforce syntax without typing anxiety.
     3. **Auditory & Contextual Passage**: Engaging short stories and informational guides followed by formative multiple-choice checks with instant positive feedback.

### 1.2 Readability & Linguistic Rules by Level

The curriculum enforces strict linguistic boundaries across all 5 tracks to guarantee age and stage appropriateness:

| Track / Level | DepEd Grade Band | CEFR Target | Avg. Sentence Length | Max Word Syllables | Phonics & Lexical Boundary | Grammatical Structures |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Pre-School & Kindergarten (Ages 4–6) | Pre-A1 | 3–6 words | 1–2 syllables | Short vowels (CVC), basic sight words, common colors, fruits, family, pets | Simple Present: Subject + Verb; Subject + Verb + Object; Demonstratives ("This is...", "I see...") |
| **Level 2** | Lower Elementary: Grades 1–3 (Ages 6–9) | A1 | 6–10 words | 1–3 syllables | Long vowels, vowel digraphs (ee, ea, oa), consonant blends (st, bl, tr), Fry 100–200 words | Present Continuous (-ing), simple prepositions (in, on, under, near), basic Wh- questions |
| **Level 3** | Upper Elementary: Grades 4–6 (Ages 9–12) | A2 | 10–15 words | 2–4 syllables | Prefixes (un-, re-), suffixes (-ful, -tion, -ly), compound words, Fry 300–500 words | Past Simple (regular & irregular), modal verbs (can, must, should), compound sentences (and, but, because) |
| **Track 4A** | Early High School: Foundations Bridge (Ages 12–16) | A1+ to A2- | 7–12 words | 2–3 syllables | Functional digital vocabulary, UI action verbs, forms, device navigation, security | Imperatives (commands & steps), modal instructions (must, need to, can), simple conditionals ("If..., then...") |
| **Track 4B** | Early High School: Standard Track (Ages 12–16) | A2 to B1 | 12–18 words | 3–5 syllables | Academic vocabulary, polite inquiry, formal school messaging, civic participation | Complex sentences with relative clauses (who, which, that), polite modals (could, would), passive voice basics |

### 5.3 Offline-First Storage & Low-Bandwidth Optimizations

1. **Lightweight Vector Assets (SVG)**:
   - All vocabulary card illustrations must be rendered as clean, flat SVGs (under 12 KB per icon) to prevent excessive data usage over 2G/3G mobile networks common in rural Philippine barangays.
2. **Audio Compression**:
   - Voice audio clips should be encoded as mono MP3 or Opus at 48 kbps, keeping pronunciation clips under 25 KB each and complete story narrations under 200 KB each.
3. **Progressive Web App (PWA) Service Worker Pre-Caching**:
   - Once a student starts a level, the Service Worker must automatically cache the complete level pack (JSON + SVGs + Audio) to allow 100% offline functionality without cell signal or WiFi.
4. **Local Progress Persistence**:
   - Save all tile-builder drag attempts, score tallies, and quiz results in browser `IndexedDB` with fallback to `localStorage`. Sync to backend servers only when an active internet connection is detected.
